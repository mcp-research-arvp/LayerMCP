# Math Public Expansion Attribution

The single-step expansion is generated from two public sources:

- DeepMind Mathematics Dataset, pinned at
  `427f45075f84b8b9774950196ad63867ca20ffb3`, Apache License 2.0.
- OpenAI GSM8K (`grade-school-math`), pinned at
  `3101c7d5072418e28b9008a6636bde82a006892c`, MIT License.

DeepMind rows record the module, interpolation split, deterministic NumPy/Python
seed, fixed `PYTHONHASHSEED`, accepted generated index, and canonical
question/answer SHA-256. GSM8K rows record the test-split line index and
canonical source-row SHA-256. The source datasets remain outside this
repository.

The adaptation preserves source questions and converts their mathematical
operation into the argument schema of one existing LayerMCP tool. Expected
answers are generated by executing that local tool. GSM8K calculator arguments
use the final contractor-authored inline calculation that produces the
published `####` answer.
